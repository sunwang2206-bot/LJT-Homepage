## About Me

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), advised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
